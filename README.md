# 🛡️ CoalGuard AI

### Predictive Smart-Governance Platform for Coal Mine Safety, Risk, Compliance & Operational Intelligence

> **CoalGuard AI** is an AI-powered decision-support and governance platform designed to help authorities and mine-management teams proactively identify safety risks, detect anomalies, monitor compliance, manage incidents, coordinate field actions, and maintain a continuous verification-based risk management workflow.

---

## 🚀 Overview

CoalGuard AI transforms fragmented coal-mine safety and compliance monitoring into a unified digital intelligence platform.

The system combines:

- 🤖 AI-based predictive risk assessment
- 🔍 Anomaly detection
- 🧠 Explainable AI
- 🗺️ 2D GIS intelligence
- 🌐 3D Digital Mine Twin
- ⚠️ Incident management
- 📋 Inspection & compliance management
- 👷 Field verification
- 📸 Evidence and GPS-based observations
- 🔄 Corrective-action workflows
- 🔔 Alerts and escalations
- 📊 Analytics and dashboards
- 🔐 Role-based access control
- 📝 Audit trails
- 📄 Reports and governance monitoring

The central concept is:

**Detect → Explain → Prioritize → Act → Verify → Recalculate**

CoalGuard AI does not replace authorized human decision-makers. AI is used to support prediction, anomaly identification, prioritization and explanation, while inspections, verification, corrective actions and regulatory decisions remain under human control.

# 🎯 Problem

Coal-mine safety information can be distributed across:

- Inspection records
- Safety observations
- Environmental conditions
- Operational indicators
- Incident reports
- Contractor activities
- Compliance records
- Field observations
- Mine locations and spatial information

When these sources are handled independently, it becomes difficult to maintain a unified view of current mine risk.
